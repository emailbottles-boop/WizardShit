Wizard Shit — Animated Wizard Cartoon & Wizard Show

Wizard Shit is an independent animated wizard cartoon and wizard show set in Wizard City. The name is playful wizard slang — "shit" means stuff, as in all the magic, spells, potions, and chaos that wizards get up to (think "wizard stuff"), and is not a comment on the show's quality. It is lovingly hand-animated by indie creators all over the world, with Adventure Time vibes. Watch the official intro, meet the wizards, and shop the merch.

What does "Wizard Shit" mean?

It is playful wizard slang. Here "shit" means stuff — as in all the magic, spells, potions, and chaos that wizards get up to. Wizard Shit is the name of an independent animated wizard cartoon set in Wizard City; the word is the title's playful vibe, not a comment on the show's quality.

Is Wizard Shit a good show?

Yes. Wizard Shit is a hand-animated indie wizard cartoon made with love by creators around the world, full of humor, magic, and Wizard City chaos, with Adventure Time vibes.
