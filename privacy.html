Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Madam Studio ("we," "us," "our") collects through the Wizard Shit website at wizardshit.store (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect personal information in two places, both of which you choose to use:

  • The email address you voluntarily submit through the "Email for Updates" form on the Site.
  • If you also write us a message in the optional box that appears beneath that form, we collect the message you type and the email address you entered with it.

We don't ask for your name, and neither form is required to browse, watch, or use any other part of the Site.

The Site does not use cookies, third-party analytics software, or tracking pixels of any kind. We don't collect device identifiers or IP addresses, and we don't build any profile of you or track you across sites.

Counting visits

We keep a simple tally of how many times each page is opened, so we can see which parts of the Site people actually use. All it records is the page address and the date — a running total per page per day. It does not record who you are, where you came from, your device, or your IP address, and it sets nothing on your computer. Two people opening the same page and one person opening it twice are indistinguishable to us.

These totals are stored in our own database, hosted for us by Cloudflare, and are not shared with anyone.

How we use your email

If you submit your email address, we use it only to send you occasional updates about Wizard Shit — things like new episodes, announcements, or merch drops. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with any third party for their own marketing purposes.

Email-signup submissions are processed for us by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. Formspree receives and stores the submitted email on our behalf so we can access it; their handling of that data is governed by Formspree's own privacy policy.

Messages you send us

If you write us a message in the optional box beneath the signup form, that message and the email address you entered are sent to our own server — not to Formspree — and stored in our database, which is hosted for us by Cloudflare. Only the people who run Wizard Shit can read it, using a password-protected admin page.

We use messages solely to read and reply to what you wrote. We don't publish them on the Site, and we don't sell, rent, or share them with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We keep messages until we no longer need them or until you ask us to delete yours.

Third-party links and embedded content

The Site links out to, and in some cases embeds content from, third-party services we don't control:

  • YouTube — for watching show videos. Where the video plays directly on our Site, we use YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (youtube-nocookie.com) to limit tracking; other links open YouTube directly.
  • Instagram — links to our show's Instagram account.
  • Donorbox — for donations, via an external link.
  • Printful — our print-on-demand merch store, via external links.

If you interact with any of these services, their own privacy policies and terms apply — we recommend reviewing them, since we aren't responsible for how they handle your information.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Some show content is intended for a general/mature audience.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any email address or message we hold at any time by contacting us at the address below. We'll remove it from our records.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at wizardshitofficial@gmail.com.
